# PAK-B02 Rolling Stock Engineering — Curriculum Architecture Design

**Date:** 11 September 2026  
**Programme:** PAK-B02 — Bachelor's-Level Rolling Stock Engineering  
**Duration:** 3 Years / 6 Semesters  
**Design Status:** Approved continuation architecture for repository implementation  
**Master PRD Baseline:** v0.2.0  
**Governance:** Architecture-before-books; degree-awarding caveat mandatory

## 1. Purpose

Define the six-semester subject architecture for PAK-B02 as the specialist rolling-stock engineering programme within the PAK Bachelor's-level portfolio. B02 must develop deeper engineering capability in railway vehicle structures, wheelsets and bogies, wheel–rail interaction, vehicle dynamics, suspension, braking and pneumatics, traction-equipment interfaces, onboard electrical/electronic systems, vehicle testing and validation, RAMS, maintenance/reliability, diagnostics, digital rolling stock, manufacturing/quality interfaces and lifecycle engineering.

This design covers programme-level and semester-level subject architecture only. It does not define chapter structures, learning outcomes, credits/ECTS, contact hours, detailed assessment weightings, or textbook prose.

## 2. Positioning in the Portfolio

PAK-B02 is the specialist vehicle-engineering programme. It sits above the applied Diploma layer and complements the broad systems-oriented PAK-B01 architecture.

### Distinction from PAK-B01

PAK-B01 remains the broad multidisciplinary railway-engineering spine. B02 goes materially deeper into vehicle-specific analysis, design, dynamics, structural behaviour, subsystem interfaces, validation, reliability, fleet/depot engineering and lifecycle decisions.

### Distinction from Diploma programmes

Diploma programmes emphasize technician/junior-engineering competence, inspection, workshop practice, maintenance procedures, component replacement, controlled diagnostics and applied operations.

PAK-B02 adds:
- higher mathematics and engineering mechanics;
- vehicle-system modelling and design reasoning;
- wheel–rail contact and dynamic behaviour;
- bogie, wheelset, bearing and suspension engineering;
- brake-system calculations and safety analysis;
- traction-drive and onboard-electrical interface engineering;
- carbody structural and crashworthiness reasoning;
- vehicle testing, validation and homologation logic;
- RAMS, reliability and lifecycle trade-offs;
- condition monitoring and predictive-maintenance analytics;
- configuration management and change control;
- multidisciplinary rolling-stock design projects;
- final-year thesis/capstone.

### Distinction from B03–B05

- B03: deeper Electrical, Signalling & Control Systems, including railway power/control/signalling specialization beyond rolling-stock interfaces.
- B04: deeper Operations, Logistics & Transport Management.
- B05: deeper Manufacturing & Maintenance Engineering, industrial processes, production systems, workshop/depot manufacturing engineering and maintenance-enterprise specialization.

B02 must remain vehicle-design, vehicle-performance and vehicle-lifecycle oriented so B03–B05 retain clear specialization value.

## 5. Academic Depth Rules

B02 is not a recoding of Diploma rolling-stock modules. Each subject must add one or more of the following beyond Diploma depth:
- mathematical derivation and quantitative analysis;
- vehicle-system modelling and simulation;
- structural or dynamic design reasoning;
- wheel–rail, suspension or running-gear performance analysis;
- brake or traction subsystem calculation;
- subsystem interface and requirements analysis;
- validation/verification and compliance reasoning;
- reliability, RAMS and lifecycle trade-offs;
- optimization and data-driven diagnostics;
- research-based analysis;
- multidisciplinary rolling-stock systems integration.

Applied practical work remains required, but it must support engineering judgement rather than replace analysis with workshop craft training.

## 6. Safety and Authorization Boundaries

The programme is academic engineering education. Completion alone does not authorize:
- train driving, dispatching, signalling operation or train movement authority;
- independent vehicle acceptance, maintenance release or return-to-service decisions;
- certified NDT sign-off or ISO 9712 certification;
- brake acceptance/release authority;
- live high-voltage or traction switching authority;
- safety-critical software approval, commissioning or independent verification authority;
- notified/designated-body, assessment-body, ECM, certification or conformity-assessment authority;
- statutory vehicle inspection or homologation approval;
- any employer/jurisdiction-specific licensed or authorized railway duty.

Safety-critical parameters, limits, test settings, procedures and acceptance/rejection criteria must come from approved current controlled sources and competent authorized organizations/personnel.

## 7. Regulatory and Technical Reference Families

Future chapter development must verify current official editions and applicability of, as relevant:
- Directive (EU) 2016/797 — railway interoperability;
- Directive (EU) 2016/798 — railway safety;
- applicable TSIs, especially LOC&PAS, WAG and relevant interfaces with CCS, ENE, INF, OPE and SRT requirements;
- Regulation (EU) 2019/779 — ECM;
- ERA Common Safety Methods, application guides and technical guidance;
- applicable EN/IEC/ISO rolling-stock, structural, dynamics, braking, electrical/electronic, RAMS, welding, fire-safety, shock/vibration, software, cybersecurity, quality, NDT and asset-management standards;
- relevant Polish railway law, national rules and competent-authority requirements;
- approved manufacturer, railway undertaking, ECM, depot and maintenance-organization controlled documents where legitimately available.

Potential reference families include current applicable editions of standards addressing vehicle dynamics/running behaviour, carbody structural strength/crashworthiness, rolling-stock fire protection, railway welding, onboard electronic equipment, vibration/shock, RAMS and quality management. Exact applicability must be revalidated before publication; proprietary standards text must not be reproduced beyond permitted use.
