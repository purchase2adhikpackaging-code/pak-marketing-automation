# PAK-D01 — Year 1 / Semester 2

**Semester:** Rolling Stock Systems & Components  
**Master PRD source:** §17.3

## Semester Objective

Build detailed knowledge of rolling-stock construction and the major mechanical, pneumatic and electrical subsystems found in freight and general railway vehicles.

| D01-204 | Wheelsets, Axles, Bearings & Axlebox Technology | Specialization Core | Theory + Workshop | D01-104, D01-105 |

## Semester Progression Logic

Semester 2 moves from vehicle-level architecture to safety- and performance-critical subsystems. Students learn how vehicle structures, bogies, running gear, wheelsets, bearings, brakes and supporting electrical/pneumatic systems interact as one rolling-stock system.

`D01-202` provides the programme's principal freight-wagon specialization and prepares students for later inspection, maintenance, compliance and workshop modules.

## Publication Rule

Each subject requires its own semester-wise print-ready subject PDF and the applicable companion publications. Safety-critical wheelset and braking chapters require source-controlled technical diagrams and explicit acceptance/authorization boundaries.
