# PAK-D01 — Year 2 / Semester 3

**Semester:** Maintenance, Inspection, Diagnostics & Repair  
**Master PRD source:** §17.4

## Semester Objective

Convert subsystem knowledge into maintenance competence through inspection, diagnostics, reliability analysis, NDT, structural repair and supervised workshop application.

## Semester Progression Logic

This semester introduces the transition from component knowledge to professional maintenance work. Students learn to diagnose faults, inspect components, interpret degradation, apply NDT principles, understand reliability/failure mechanisms and carry out controlled repair practice.

`D01-306` acts as the integration module for freight wagon, bogie and wheelset inspection and will later contain formal practical competency checkpoints.

## Publication Rule

Every subject requires a print-ready textbook. Workshop/NDT/welding/practicum subjects also require controlled practical manuals, student journals/logbooks, technical drawings, defect illustrations, inspection diagrams and instructor assessment records as applicable.
