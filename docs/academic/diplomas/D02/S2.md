# PAK-D02 — Year 1 / Semester 2

**Semester:** Mechanical Systems, Manufacturing & Railway Applications  
**Master PRD source:** §18.3  
**Publishing governance:** `../../governance/PRINT_READY_PUBLISHING_STANDARD.md`

## Semester Objective

Develop core mechanical engineering knowledge in structures, machines, thermofluids and manufacturing while applying it to railway components and vehicle mechanical systems.

## Semester Progression Logic

Semester 2 turns basic engineering knowledge into component-level mechanical understanding. Students study stress/deformation, mechanisms/machine elements, fluid power, manufacturing, welding and dimensional quality. `D02-205` connects structural mechanics and machine elements to underframes, couplers, bogies, suspension and running gear; `D02-206` establishes the measurement/tolerance discipline required for later inspection and overhaul.

## Publication Rule

Each subject gets its own print-ready PDF; drawing-heavy and workshop-heavy subjects must include high-quality engineering figures, worked examples, practical manuals and student record formats where required.
