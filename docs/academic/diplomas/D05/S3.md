**Semester:** Railway Welding, Inspection, Process Control & QA/QC

## Semester Objective

Apply manufacturing and welding fundamentals to railway-quality environments with controlled procedures, advanced inspection, traceability and production-quality systems.

## Semester Progression Logic

Semester 3 moves from generic manufacturing to controlled railway production. `D05-301` introduces railway welding quality frameworks and formal qualification/certification concepts without claiming course equivalence; `D05-302` develops procedure-based welding/process control; `D05-303` develops method selection/defect characterization; `D05-305` formalizes material-to-production-to-inspection traceability and NCR/CAPA; and `D05-306` integrates production and quality in supervised practice.

## Publication Rule

Every subject requires its own print-ready PDF. Welding/NDT/QA subjects also require controlled procedure examples, weld/defect drawings, inspection diagrams, records/forms, practical journals and instructor-only acceptance/assessment guidance where applicable.
