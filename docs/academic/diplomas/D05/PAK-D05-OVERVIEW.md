## Programme Purpose

PAK-D05 prepares manufacturing technicians, welding/fabrication technicians, production-quality personnel, inspectors and junior manufacturing professionals for railway workshops and supply-chain environments. The programme combines materials science, manufacturing technology, welding metallurgy, fabrication, dimensional metrology, NDT, quality assurance, process control, production planning, traceability and railway-specific manufacturing awareness.

Students progress from materials, drawing, metrology and safe workshop fundamentals in Semester 1; through machining, forming, welding, fabrication, quality and NDT fundamentals in Semester 2; into railway welding, advanced process control, weld inspection, railway-component manufacturing and formal QA/QC systems in Semester 3; and finally into advanced fabrication/repair, production engineering, audits, digital quality and industry application in Semester 4.

This diploma provides academic and practical preparation but does **not** itself replace personnel qualification/certification, welding coordination authorization, NDT certification, production certification, manufacturer approval or railway conformity requirements.

## Curriculum Design Principles

1. Metallurgy before welding judgement.
2. Drawing-to-product traceability.
3. Process discipline over craft alone.
4. Quality built into production.
5. NDT competency boundaries.
6. Railway-specific quality awareness including current applicable railway welding frameworks.
7. Safety-critical segregation.
8. Digital manufacturing readiness.
9. Repair as controlled engineering.

## Shared-Module Strategy

D05 may reuse D01/D02 foundations in materials/corrosion, drawing/documentation, workshop safety, basic metrology, manufacturing awareness, NDT fundamentals and quality/traceability only where outcomes match. Independent/expanded D05 depth is required for welding metallurgy/weldability, welding process control, railway welding quality frameworks, fabrication/fixtures, advanced weld inspection, SPC/process capability, NCR/CAPA, auditing/supplier quality and robotic/digital manufacturing quality.

## Technical, Standards & Competence Baseline

Future chapters must verify current applicable railway welding, welding quality, NDT, metrology, drawing/GD&T, quality-management, EU railway and Polish requirements. Academic knowledge, supervised training, personnel certification, organization/process certification and actual product acceptance/release must be clearly separated.

Safety-critical welds, wheelset/bogie structures, load-bearing repairs and other critical components cannot be released based on educational exercises alone.

## Chapter-Decomposition Readiness

Each subject must later define chapter sequence, material/process prerequisites, process diagrams/manufacturing flows, welding symbols/drawing examples, theory/lab/workshop split, weld/fabrication exercises, metrology/NDT tasks, traceability records, NCR/CAPA/audit cases, learning outcomes, assessment links, standards/reference families, certification boundaries, safety flags and print-ready publication deliverables.
