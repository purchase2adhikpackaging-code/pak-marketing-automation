# PAK-D05 — Year 2 / Semester 4

**Semester:** Advanced Production Quality, Repair, Auditing & Industry Application  
**Master PRD source:** §21.5  
**Publishing governance:** `../../governance/PRINT_READY_PUBLISHING_STANDARD.md`

## Semester Objective

Integrate advanced fabrication and repair control with production engineering, supplier quality, auditing, digital inspection and real-world railway manufacturing projects.

| Code | Subject | Classification | Orientation | Prerequisite |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D05-401 | Advanced Railway Fabrication, Weld Repair, Rework & Production Troubleshooting | Specialization Core | Theory + Workshop / Case Study | D05-301, D05-302, D05-303, D05-306 |
| D05-402 | Production Planning, Lean Manufacturing, Industrial Engineering & Supply Quality | Core / Specialization | Theory + Planning / Optimization Exercises | D05-201, D05-205, D05-305 |
| D05-403 | Quality Management Systems, Auditing & Railway Supplier Quality | Core / Quality | Theory + Audit Simulation + Case Study | D05-305, D05-306 |
| D05-404 | Advanced Metrology, Measurement Systems, Process Capability & Digital Quality | Specialization / Quality | Metrology Lab + Data Analysis | D05-205, D05-303 |
| D05-405 | Manufacturing Automation, Robotic Welding, Sustainable Production & Emerging Processes | Specialization | Theory + Automation Demonstration / Project | D05-201, D05-202, D05-205 |
| D05-406 | Industry Practicum & Railway Manufacturing/Welding/Quality Capstone | Practical / Capstone | Industry + Project + Competency Assessment | Completion of Semesters 1–3; co-requisite D05-401 to D05-405 |

## Semester Progression Logic

The final semester treats manufacturing quality as a complete controlled system rather than final inspection. `D05-401` covers controlled repair/rework; `D05-402` connects production flow/capacity/waste/supplier performance; `D05-403` introduces audit/supplier-quality thinking; `D05-404` deepens measurement-system/process-capability analysis; `D05-405` covers automation/robotic welding/digital quality/sustainability; and `D05-406` integrates drawing/process interpretation, safety, inspection planning, traceability, nonconformance handling and technical presentation.

## Publication Rule

Every subject requires a print-ready semester PDF. Advanced repair, audit, automation and capstone subjects must also map controlled case records, process diagrams, digital-quality examples, project journals, audit forms and assessment rubrics.
