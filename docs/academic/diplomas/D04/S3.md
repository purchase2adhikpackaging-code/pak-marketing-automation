# PAK-D04 — Year 2 / Semester 3

**Semester:** Traffic Control, Disruption, Safety & Service Optimization  
**Master PRD source:** §20.4  
**Publishing governance:** `../../governance/PRINT_READY_PUBLISHING_STANDARD.md`

## Semester Objective

Develop the ability to analyze and manage operational performance, disruption, risk, logistics flows and passenger service through simulations, case studies and structured decision-making.

| Code | Subject | Classification | Orientation | Prerequisite |

| D04-301 | Railway Control Centre Operations & Traffic Regulation | Specialization Core | Theory + Traffic Simulation | D04-201, D04-202 |

## Semester Progression Logic

Semester 3 shifts from planned operations to real-time/performance-sensitive operations. Students study conceptual traffic regulation, disruption propagation, safety/risk/human performance, freight planning and passenger demand/revenue. `D04-306` integrates timetable conflicts, yard movements, rolling-stock constraints, delay recovery, passenger information and freight priorities in a controlled simulation without granting real-world dispatch authority.

## Publication Rule

Each subject requires a print-ready textbook. Simulation/practicum modules also require scenario packs, student workbooks/journals, data tables, process maps, instructor controls and assessment rubrics.
