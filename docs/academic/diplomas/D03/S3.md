# PAK-D03 — Year 2 / Semester 3

**Semester:** Power Electronics, Train Control, Protection & Diagnostics  
**Master PRD source:** §19.4  
**Publishing governance:** `../../governance/PRINT_READY_PUBLISHING_STANDARD.md`

## Semester Objective

Develop applied competence in modern traction power conversion, onboard control, train communication/signalling interfaces, protection and systematic electrical troubleshooting.

## Semester Progression Logic

Semester 3 focuses on technologies that make railway electrical systems controllable and diagnosable: semiconductor power conversion, motor-drive control, regenerative braking, onboard communication and train-control interfaces. `D03-305` formalizes safe troubleshooting; `D03-306` integrates wiring, control circuits, measurement and fault finding. `D03-303` remains signalling/train-protection interface education and does not confer safety-critical signalling authority.

## Publication Rule

Each subject requires a print-ready textbook; diagnostics/practicum subjects also require controlled lab/workshop manuals, fault trees, network diagrams, wiring schematics, journal evidence and instructor assessment rubrics.
