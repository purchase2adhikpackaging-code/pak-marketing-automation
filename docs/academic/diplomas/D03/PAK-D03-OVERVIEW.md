## Programme Purpose

PAK-D03 prepares railway electrical technicians and junior engineering professionals who can understand, test, inspect, maintain and troubleshoot electrical and control systems used in railway vehicles and related technical environments. It combines electrical/electronic fundamentals with traction machines, power conversion, onboard auxiliaries, control/instrumentation, train communication, signalling interfaces, protection, diagnostics and maintenance practice.

The curriculum begins with electrical science, electronics, mathematics, drawings, measurement and safe laboratory practice; advances into machines, power systems, control engineering, traction and onboard systems; then develops power electronics, train-control/signalling interfaces, protection and diagnostics before culminating in advanced traction, TCMS/communications, maintenance engineering, interoperability awareness and an industry capstone.

## Curriculum Design Principles

1. Electrical safety first.
2. Fundamentals before traction.
3. Power and control integration.
4. Measurement-led diagnostics.
5. Onboard railway relevance.
6. Interface awareness without false authorization claims.
7. Digital readiness through PLC, embedded control, communication buses, TCMS, data logging and predictive diagnostics.
8. Compliance traceability.

## Shared-Module Strategy

Reuse common content only where level/scope/outcomes match, especially railway fundamentals, safety, technical documentation, maintenance/reliability foundations, quality/traceability and generic measurement/condition-monitoring concepts. D03 requires independent or expanded treatment for circuits, electronics, machines/transformers, power electronics, traction drives, protection/insulation diagnostics, PLC/control, auxiliaries, networks/TCMS and EMC/compliance.

## Regulatory & Safety Baseline

Use current interoperability/safety legislation, applicable rolling-stock/energy/control-command TSIs, ERA guidance, EN/IEC/ISO standards, Polish electrical/railway requirements, and authorized manufacturer schematics/manuals where legitimate.

Educational diagrams and thresholds must not be presented as universal railway acceptance limits. High-voltage, traction-power and safety-critical control/signalling practical work requires explicit facility, competence and authorization controls.
