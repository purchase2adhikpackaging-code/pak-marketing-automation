# PAK-D03 — Year 2 / Semester 4

**Semester:** Advanced Control, Maintenance, Compliance & Industry Application  
**Master PRD source:** §19.5  
**Publishing governance:** `../../governance/PRINT_READY_PUBLISHING_STANDARD.md`

## Semester Objective

Integrate traction and control-system knowledge with TCMS, advanced diagnostics, maintenance engineering, quality/compliance and real-world electrical project work.

| Code | Subject | Classification | Orientation | Prerequisite |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D03-401 | Advanced Traction Drives, Energy Management & Electrical Braking | Specialization Core | Theory + Simulation / Laboratory | D03-301, D03-302 |
| D03-402 | Train Control & Management Systems (TCMS), Vehicle Networks & Remote Diagnostics | Specialization Core | Theory + Network / Diagnostic Lab | D03-304, D03-305 |
| D03-403 | Railway Electrical Maintenance, Reliability & Condition-Based Maintenance | Core / Specialization | Theory + Laboratory + Case Study | D03-305, D03-306 |
| D03-404 | Electrical Safety, EMC, Quality, Documentation & Railway Compliance | Core / Specialization | Theory + Compliance / Documentation Exercises | D03-202, D03-305 |
| D03-405 | Advanced Automation, Data Acquisition & Predictive Diagnostics | Specialization | PLC / DAQ Lab + Project | D03-203, D03-204, D03-304, D03-305 |
| D03-406 | Industry Practicum & Electrical/Control Systems Capstone Project | Practical / Capstone | Industry + Project + Competency Assessment | Completion of Semesters 1–3; co-requisite D03-401 to D03-405 |

## Semester Progression Logic

The final semester moves from subsystem troubleshooting to system-level electrical maintenance and engineering support. It deepens traction drives/energy flow, TCMS/network architecture, reliability/maintenance strategy, EMC/safety/compliance and data-supported automation/diagnostics. `D03-406` integrates design interpretation, safe testing, troubleshooting, technical records and system reasoning.

## Publication Rule

Every subject requires a print-ready PDF. TCMS/automation/capstone subjects must additionally map network/diagnostic diagrams, project journals, simulation/lab manuals, technical-report templates and assessment rubrics.
