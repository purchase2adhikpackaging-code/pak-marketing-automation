## Programme Purpose

PAK-B02 is the specialist rolling-stock engineering programme in the PAK Bachelor's-level portfolio. It develops deeper engineering capability in railway vehicle architecture, structures, running gear, wheelsets and bogies, vehicle dynamics, suspension, braking and pneumatics, traction-equipment interfaces, onboard electrical/electronic systems, carbody systems, testing and validation, RAMS, diagnostics, digital rolling stock, maintenance optimization and fleet lifecycle engineering.

The programme sits above the applied Diploma layer. Diploma programmes emphasize technician and junior-engineering competence, workshop practice, inspection, controlled diagnostics and prescribed maintenance activity. B02 adds higher mathematics, quantitative vehicle analysis, structural and dynamic modelling, subsystem design/interface reasoning, validation/verification, RAMS, reliability, lifecycle trade-offs, optimization, configuration management, research methods and multidisciplinary vehicle design.

B02 complements rather than duplicates the other Bachelor's programmes. B01 remains the broad multidisciplinary railway-engineering spine; B03 retains deeper electrical/signalling/control specialization; B04 retains deeper operations/logistics specialization; and B05 retains deeper manufacturing/maintenance-enterprise specialization.

## Curriculum Design Principles

1. **Engineering science before vehicle specialization** — mathematics, mechanics, materials, electrical science, thermo-fluid science and computation precede advanced vehicle work.
2. **Vehicle systems integration over isolated components** — structures, running gear, braking, traction, onboard systems and human/safety interfaces are treated as an integrated vehicle.
3. **Dynamics and wheel–rail behaviour as core engineering disciplines** — running stability, suspension, ride, wheel–rail contact and vehicle performance progress to advanced analysis.
4. **Analysis and design above Diploma depth** — subjects add mathematical modelling, simulation, design, validation or lifecycle reasoning beyond applied maintenance treatment.
5. **Safety/RAMS and validation by design** — reliability, maintainability, hazards, testing, verification and evidence-based assurance are built into progression.
6. **Lifecycle and fleet thinking** — design, manufacturing interfaces, operation, maintenance, renewal, depot strategy and asset performance are linked.
7. **Digital rolling stock readiness** — TCMS awareness, sensors, data analytics, predictive maintenance and defensive cybersecurity support modern vehicle engineering.
8. **Practical engineering supports analytical reasoning** — controlled labs, workshops and simulations reinforce engineering judgement without implying operational authority.
9. **Regulatory traceability without false authorization claims** — EU/Polish requirements and technical standards are referenced without implying certification, homologation or statutory authority.
10. **Research, professional practice and capstone integration** — the final year requires defensible engineering evidence, supervised industry exposure and independent multidisciplinary work.

## Specialization Boundaries

- **B01 = broad multidisciplinary Railway Engineering**
- **B03 = deeper Electrical, Signalling & Control Systems**
- **B04 = deeper Operations, Logistics & Transport Management**
- **B05 = deeper Manufacturing & Maintenance Engineering**

B02 therefore concentrates on vehicle design, performance, validation and lifecycle integration. Electrical/control, operations and industrial/manufacturing topics are included only to the depth needed for rolling-stock subsystem integration and interfaces.

## Academic Depth Rule

B02 subjects must not simply re-code Diploma rolling-stock content. Each subject must add one or more of the following where relevant: mathematical derivation, quantitative analysis, vehicle-system modelling, structural/dynamic design reasoning, wheel–rail or suspension analysis, brake/traction calculations, subsystem interface analysis, verification/validation, RAMS/lifecycle trade-offs, optimization, data-driven diagnostics, research-based analysis or multidisciplinary vehicle systems integration.

## Safety & Authorization Boundary

PAK-B02 is academic engineering education. Completion **does not authorize** train driving, dispatching or train movement; independent vehicle acceptance, maintenance release or return-to-service; certified NDT sign-off; brake acceptance/release; live high-voltage or traction switching; safety-critical software approval or commissioning; homologation approval; ECM/notified/designated/assessment-body/conformity authority; statutory vehicle inspection/sign-off; or any employer/jurisdiction-specific licensed railway duty.

Safety-critical parameters, limits, test settings, procedures and acceptance/rejection criteria must come from approved current controlled sources and competent authorized organizations/personnel.
