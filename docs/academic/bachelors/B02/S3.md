## Progression & Systems Integration Logic

Semester 3 is the transition from general vehicle-engineering science to rolling-stock subsystem engineering. Vehicle architecture, running gear, dynamics, braking/pneumatics and traction/onboard systems are taught as connected subsystems with explicit requirements and interfaces. B02-306 integrates measurements, controlled component demonstrations, calculations and simulation so practical work supports engineering reasoning rather than implying maintenance or operational authority.

## Safety & Authorization Controls

Semester 3 work does **not authorize** brake acceptance/release, train movement, independent vehicle inspection, maintenance release/return-to-service, live high-voltage work, traction switching or intervention in live operational TCMS/control systems. Safety-relevant demonstrations require controlled educational conditions and competent supervision.

## Future Technical Visual & Publication Requirements

Future materials must include vehicle architecture/interface diagrams, bogie and wheelset assemblies, axlebox/bearing diagrams, suspension schematics, introductory vehicle-dynamics models, brake and pneumatic circuits, door-system diagrams, traction-drive block diagrams, onboard electrical architecture, requirements traceability examples, simulation plots and practical worksheets.
