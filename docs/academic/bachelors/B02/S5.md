## Vehicle Integration & Lifecycle Logic

Semester 5 moves from subsystem competence to whole-vehicle engineering. Students connect requirements and configuration management with digital/TCMS interfaces, test and validation evidence, regulatory traceability, manufacturing/quality interfaces, RAMS, human factors and lifecycle decisions. B02-506 requires multidisciplinary trade-offs across vehicle performance, safety, maintainability, compliance and technical evidence.

## Authority & Cybersecurity Boundaries

B02-502 treats cybersecurity defensively within controlled educational environments and does not authorize offensive access, penetration of real rolling-stock systems or intervention in operational TCMS/vehicle networks. B02-503 teaches test, validation, homologation and compliance reasoning but does not confer notified/designated/assessment-body, homologation or certification authority. B02-504 does not confer certified welding, inspection or production-release authority. B02-505 does not confer independent safety-assessment or maintenance-release authority.
