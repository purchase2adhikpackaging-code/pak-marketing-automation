# PAK-B02 — Semester 1

## Engineering & Rolling Stock Foundations

**Curriculum Status:** Subject Architecture v1 — Chapter decomposition pending  
**Semester Objective:** Establish the mathematical, scientific, drawing, materials, electrical/electronic and rolling-stock foundations required for specialist railway-vehicle engineering.

## Progression Logic

Semester 1 creates six foundations: quantitative reasoning; mechanics and dynamics; engineering representation/CAD; materials/metallurgy/manufacturing literacy; electrical/electronic fundamentals; and railway-vehicle/safety vocabulary. Later B02 subjects must build analytically on these foundations rather than repeat introductory Diploma-level rolling-stock treatment.

## Practical / Laboratory Expectations

Planned learning modes include CAD exercises, engineering measurements and demonstrations, materials/metallurgy identification, manufacturing-process demonstrations, de-energized or low-voltage electrical/electronic laboratory activities, rolling-stock subsystem demonstrations and structured technical-communication exercises.

Safety-critical operational and maintenance-release procedures remain awareness-only. Electrical practical work defaults to de-energized or purpose-built low-voltage educational assets unless a separately approved facility, competent supervision and authorization framework exists.

## Future Technical Visual & Publication Requirements

Future chapter/publication design must include mathematics worked examples, statics/dynamics diagrams, engineering drawings and CAD figures, metallurgy/material-process visuals, basic circuit diagrams, rolling-stock architecture and component maps, workbook problem sets and controlled laboratory sheets. Chapter titles are intentionally not defined at this stage.

## Architecture-before-books Gate

No subject textbook prose may begin until the corresponding B02 subject chapter architecture is approved or frozen.
