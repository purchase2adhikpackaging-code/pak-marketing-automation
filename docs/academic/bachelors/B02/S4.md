**Curriculum Status:** Subject Architecture v1 — Chapter decomposition pending

## Progression & Integration Logic

Semester 4 raises B02 from first-level subsystem understanding to performance modelling, interface analysis, structural/safety reasoning and reliability engineering. B02-401/B02-402 connect running gear and wheel–rail behaviour to ride/stability performance; B02-403/B02-404 deepen safety-critical brake and traction/onboard interfaces; B02-405 integrates structural, passenger-environment and fire-safety considerations; B02-406 establishes the reliability and condition foundation for lifecycle engineering in Semesters 5–6.

## Safety & Authorization Controls

NDT activity is educational and does not confer ISO 9712 certification or inspection sign-off. Brake and pneumatic demonstrations do not confer vehicle acceptance or brake-release authority. Electrical/traction practical work remains within approved educational facilities and does not grant live high-voltage or traction-switching authority. Reliability analysis does not confer independent maintenance release or return-to-service authority.

## Future Technical Visual & Publication Requirements

Future materials must include bogie/wheelset load paths, wheel–rail contact diagrams, suspension models, stability/ride simulation plots, brake-performance schematics, pneumatic timing diagrams, traction/auxiliary energy-flow diagrams, carbody structural/crashworthiness illustrations, HVAC/fire-safety system maps, reliability block diagrams, FMEA/FMECA-style teaching examples, NDT/condition-monitoring plots and controlled lab sheets.
