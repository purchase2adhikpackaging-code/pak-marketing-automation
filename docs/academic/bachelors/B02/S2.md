# PAK-B02 — Semester 2

## Core Vehicle Engineering Sciences

**Curriculum Status:** Subject Architecture v1 — Chapter decomposition pending

## Progression Logic

Semester 2 moves from introductory foundations into structural response, thermo-fluid behaviour, electrical machines/power electronics/control, manufacturing quality and computational engineering. B02-201 and B02-206 support later dynamics, simulation, reliability analytics and optimization; B02-202 to B02-205 provide the engineering-science basis for vehicle structures, bogies, brakes, traction interfaces and industrialization.

## Practical / Laboratory & Safety Expectations

Learning modes include controlled mechanics/material demonstrations, fluid and pneumatic rigs, de-energized/low-voltage machines and control laboratories, dimensional/metrology exercises, process-quality studies and programming/data-analysis labs. The semester does not authorize traction switching, live high-voltage work, certified inspection or intervention in operational safety-critical vehicle systems.
