## Professional Practice & Capstone Controls

Internship activity must be governed by the host site's competence, supervision, access and authorization rules. Student status does **not authorize** independent safety-critical operational, signalling, electrical, inspection, release or certification duties.

The final capstone/thesis must define a defensible engineering problem, assumptions and data provenance, quantitative analysis, subsystem/interface and safety considerations, technical/regulatory reference traceability, verification reasoning, limitations and clear professional technical communication. It should demonstrate Bachelor's-level engineering reasoning rather than merely document workshop activity.

## Future Publication & Assessment Implications

Future chapter/outcome phases must define systems-interface matrices, asset-strategy models, risk and verification/validation workflows, research-method templates, literature-review guidance, engineering-ethics cases, internship logbook architecture, capstone proposal/report templates and marking-rubric structures. Detailed assessment weights are intentionally not assigned in this architecture phase.
