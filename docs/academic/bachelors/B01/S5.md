## Network & Lifecycle Integration Logic

Semester 5 moves from subsystem competence to whole-railway engineering. Students connect operations and capacity with modelling/optimization, interoperability and maintenance governance, digital systems, engineering economics, sustainability and multidisciplinary design review.

## Authority & Cybersecurity Boundaries

B01-501 teaches engineering understanding of operations but does **not authorize** dispatching, signalling operation, train-control decisions or train movement. B01-503 teaches regulatory/compliance reasoning but does not confer ECM, assessment-body, certification or conformity-assessment authority. B01-504 treats cybersecurity defensively within railway engineering; it does not authorize offensive access, penetration of real railway systems or operational-system intervention. B01-506 uses controlled assumptions, datasets and simulations rather than live operational changes.
