## Progression & Systems Integration Logic

Semester 3 is the transition from general engineering science to railway subsystem engineering. Infrastructure, rolling stock, traction/energy, signalling/control and braking are taught as connected systems with explicit interfaces. B01-306 integrates measurements, calculations, controlled workshop/laboratory activities and simulation so practical work supports engineering reasoning rather than implying operational authority.

## Safety & Authorization Controls

Semester 3 academic work does **not authorize** operational signalling decisions, traction-power switching, live high-voltage operational work, independent brake acceptance/release, train movement authority, certified NDT, structural acceptance or maintenance release-to-service. Any demonstration involving safety-relevant equipment requires controlled educational conditions and competent supervision.

## Future Technical Visual & Publication Requirements

Future materials must include track cross-sections, structural/load diagrams, geotechnical profiles, vehicle-dynamics and running-gear diagrams, traction-energy block diagrams, signalling logic/interface diagrams, brake and pneumatic schematics, simulation plots, systems-interface maps and practical worksheets.
