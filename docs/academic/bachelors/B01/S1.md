# PAK-B01 — Semester 1

## Engineering & Railway Foundations

**Curriculum Status:** Subject Architecture v1 — Chapter decomposition pending  
**Semester Objective:** Establish the mathematical, scientific, drawing, materials, electrical/electronic and railway-system foundations required for multidisciplinary railway engineering.

## Progression Logic

Semester 1 establishes six parallel foundations: quantitative reasoning; mechanics and engineering physics; engineering representation and CAD; materials/process understanding; electrical/electronic literacy; and railway-system, safety and technical-communication vocabulary. Later B01 subjects must build analytically on these foundations rather than repeat introductory Diploma-level treatment.

## Practical / Laboratory Expectations

Planned learning modes include CAD exercises, engineering measurements and demonstrations, materials identification and manufacturing-process demonstrations, de-energized or low-voltage electrical/electronic laboratory activities, railway subsystem demonstrations and structured technical-communication exercises.

Safety-critical operational procedures remain awareness-only. Practical electrical work defaults to de-energized or purpose-built low-voltage educational assets unless a separately approved facility, competent supervision and authorization framework exists.

## Future Technical Visual & Publication Requirements

Future chapter/publication design must include mathematics worked examples, statics/dynamics diagrams, engineering drawings and CAD figures, materials microstructure/process visuals, basic circuit diagrams, railway system maps, workbook problem sets and controlled laboratory sheets. Chapter titles are intentionally not defined at this stage.

## Architecture-before-books Gate

No subject textbook prose may begin until the corresponding B01 subject chapter architecture is approved or frozen.
