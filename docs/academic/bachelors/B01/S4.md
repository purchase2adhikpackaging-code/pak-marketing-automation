## Progression & Integration Logic

Semester 4 raises the technical depth from first-level subsystem understanding to modelling, interface analysis, reliability and engineering assurance. B01-406 integrates subsystem interfaces, hazards, human factors, RAMS concepts, safety-lifecycle reasoning and evidence-based engineering decisions, creating the systems foundation for network/lifecycle integration in Semester 5.

## Safety & Authorization Controls

NDT activity is educational and does not confer ISO 9712 certification or inspection sign-off. Signalling/interlocking simulations do not confer design, testing, commissioning or operational authority. Traction-power and power-electronics practical work remains within authorized educational facilities and does not grant switching or live high-voltage authority. Reliability and maintenance analysis does not grant independent maintenance release.

## Future Technical Visual & Publication Requirements

Future materials must include advanced vehicle-dynamics models, infrastructure condition maps, power-electronics/energy-flow diagrams, signalling/interlocking logic diagrams, reliability block diagrams, FMEA/FMECA-style teaching examples, condition-monitoring plots, system/interface diagrams, safety/risk workflows and controlled lab/simulation sheets.
