# PAK Regulatory, Source-Control and Academic QA Rules

**Status:** Governance mirror for fast retrieval  
**Source authority:** Master PRD regulatory, standards, safety and QA rules  
**Rule:** This mirror is subordinate to the master PRD and must be synchronized when global governance changes.

## 1. Current-Source Verification

Safety-critical, regulatory and compliance content must be verified against the current applicable source at chapter-writing and again at publication/revision time. Do not assume a previously cited law, TSI, ERA document, EN/ISO standard, manufacturer instruction or national rule remains current.

Priority source families include, where applicable:

- EU railway interoperability legislation;
- EU railway safety legislation;
- current applicable TSIs and consolidated amendments;
- European Union Agency for Railways (ERA) guidance and technical documentation;
- Polish railway and workplace-safety requirements;
- approved infrastructure-manager, railway-undertaking, keeper, ECM or manufacturer documentation;
- applicable EN, IEC and ISO standards;
- recognized technical and academic references appropriate to the subject.

## 2. Proprietary Standards and Copyright

Proprietary standards, textbooks and manufacturer manuals must not be reproduced in full unless PAK has explicit lawful permission. Educational material should explain requirements in original language and cite/reference the governing source.

## 3. Approved Technical Data Rule

Educational examples must never be presented as universal safety-critical acceptance criteria. Asset-/process-specific values such as dimensions, wear limits, torque values, pressures, electrical settings, software parameters, brake criteria, weld/NDT acceptance limits or release requirements must be traceable to the exact governing approved source.

## 4. Qualification and Authorization Boundaries

Academic course completion does not automatically grant statutory, employer or certification authority. Books and assessments must explicitly distinguish:

- academic knowledge;
- supervised training practice;
- personnel certification/qualification;
- organization/process certification;
- employer/jurisdiction authorization;
- product/vehicle/component acceptance or release to service.

This is especially important for train driving, signalling/dispatching, high-voltage work, brake acceptance, wheelset release, safety-critical welding, NDT sign-off, software/configuration changes, dangerous-goods specialist duties and maintenance release.

## 5. Regulatory Traceability Metadata

Each future chapter/publication that contains regulated or safety-critical material must record:

- source title/identifier;
- edition, issue, amendment or consolidated version checked;
- access/review date;
- applicability/context;
- affected chapter/topic;
- reviewer where formal technical review is required.

## 6. Safety-Critical Visual Rule

Educational diagrams, drawings, schematics, defect examples and procedures that are not approved controlled instructions must be labelled **Illustrative / Training Use Only** where there is a realistic risk of confusion.

## 7. Academic QA Gates

Before full book production:

1. programme/module or semester subject architecture approved;
2. chapter architecture approved;
3. learning outcomes mapped;
4. practical/lab/workshop scope mapped;
5. assessment placeholders mapped;
6. visual/diagram requirements mapped;
7. regulatory/reference families identified;
8. safety-critical and authorization boundaries flagged;
9. publication deliverables declared;
10. print-layout requirements known.

Before final publication:

1. academic content review;
2. technical/safety review;
3. regulatory/reference verification;
4. citation and copyright review;
5. visual/diagram QA;
6. copy-editing and terminology consistency;
7. assessment/rubric review;
8. pagination/index/TOC verification;
9. print proof review;
10. version and approval sign-off.

## 8. Degree-Level Caveat

Bachelor's-level, Master's-level and postgraduate curricula can be architected by PAK, but recognized/protected degree titles may only be marketed or awarded where the applicable Polish legal/accreditation/authorization framework or authorized higher-education partner permits it.

## 9. Change Control

A change to a global regulatory or QA rule must be made in the master PRD first, then synchronized into this mirror and any affected modular programme files.
